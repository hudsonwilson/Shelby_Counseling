Meet Shelby

Hi, I'm Shelby Wilson, a Fort Worth therapist for teens, couples, and couples-to-be.

Practicing at Fort Worth Counseling and Intervention (FWCAI)

I have always been intrigued by patterns and people, how we think, how we relate, and the generational habits that shape us for better or for worse. That curiosity led me to an education dedicated to understanding and helping others. I received my BS in Human Development and Family Science from Auburn University, then went on to earn my Master's in Marriage and Family Therapy from Liberty University.

My therapeutic approach is systemic and narrative based, with a strong foundation in family systems and a lot of collaboration with each client along the way. Simply put, I want to hear and explore every layer of your story. I want you to feel free to brain dump every spiraling thought, prompting question, and messy narrative that enters your mind, trusting that you have a listening ear striving to understand and eager to unpack it all together.

As a Christian, my faith shapes and enriches the way I view healing and growth. I balance warm encouragement with compassionate accountability, affirming the strengths you already carry while gently challenging you in areas of personal growth so you can bridge the gaps toward fulfillment. I offer a flexible therapeutic environment tailored to what matters most to you. If incorporating your faith feels meaningful, we can make that a core part of our sessions. If you prefer a purely clinical focus, we'll stick entirely to evidence-based practice.

Life is full of triumphs and trials, and I truly believe that no one is too lost or too burdened to navigate through hardship and find resilience, hope, and peaceful reconciliation. When life feels overwhelming, we often get stuck and don't know what comes next. Therapy can be that first step. I'm here to help individuals, couples, and families uncover problematic patterns, build stronger communication, and replace dysfunctional habits with constructive actions.

My intention is that you walk out more equipped and empowered to encounter conflict with strong communication. Therapy isn't about a quick fix, but about deepening your self-awareness so lasting growth can unfold. You don't have to figure it out alone, and I would be honored to walk alongside you.

How I Can Help

Focused, caring support in three areas

Every family and relationship looks different. Here's where I focus my training and attention as a Fort Worth therapist at FWCAI, serving clients in Aledo, Weatherford, Southwest Fort Worth, Benbrook, and beyond.

Adolescent Therapy

I work with teens across Fort Worth, Aledo, and Benbrook on anxiety, identity questions, school stress, and family conflict, meeting young people where they actually are rather than where a textbook says they should be.

Couples Counseling

I help couples throughout Fort Worth and Weatherford rebuild trust, communicate without escalating, and remember why they chose each other in the first place.

Premarital Counseling

I prepare engaged couples in Fort Worth, Aledo, and Southwest Fort Worth for a strong start, covering communication, finances, family expectations, and conflict habits before they settle in for good.

Many of my Fort Worth clients ask about Christian counseling. If your faith, whether it's rooted in a bible church or another tradition, is something you'd like woven into our work, I'm glad to include it.
